# Security Policy

Security reports concerning the EACE framework itself are welcome.

## Scope

- Defects in the evidence ledger, governor, verifier, or synthetic environment that undermine the research goals of EACE.
- Failures of fail-closed behaviour in the v0.2 verifier.

## Out of scope

- Unauthorized testing against third-party systems.
- Exploitation of production Android devices or services that the researcher does not own or have explicit authorization to test.
- Requests for assistance in building real-world exploits, privilege-escalation chains, or persistence mechanisms.

## Policy

Experiments must remain within owned or explicitly authorized environments.  
The Android P0 record is a read-only capability probe; the repository does not provide instructions for kernel exploitation, SELinux bypass, credential extraction, or destructive modification.
